Thank You for Inspiring the Tinkers!

Dear Akansha Ma'am,


On this Teacher's Day, we wanted to do something special to show you how much we appreciate everything you do.


You don't just teach us; you give us the freedom to experiment, make mistakes, and build amazing things in the Tinkering Lab.


Whether it's a circuit that won't work, a robot acting up, or a crazy new idea we want to pitch, you are always there with endless patience and encouragement.


Thank you for pushing us beyond our limits, for believing in our potential, and for being the absolute best mentor we could ever ask for.


You make learning feel like an adventure, and because of you, we know we can engineer a better tomorrow!

With gratitude, The Tinkers
